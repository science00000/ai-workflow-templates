FAQ

Do I need an OpenAI API key?

No. The bot runs entirely on local LLMs via Ollama. Zero API costs. You can also use OpenAI-compatible endpoints if preferred.

What are the system requirements?

Docker + 4GB RAM minimum (8GB recommended for the LLM). Works on Mac, Linux, and Windows.

Can I use it commercially?

Yes — MIT License. Use it for your own projects, clients, or products. No attribution required.

How do I customize the knowledge base?

Drop your .md, .txt, or .json files into data/knowledge-base/ and hit the rebuild endpoint. The bot learns from your content automatically.

Does it support Vietnamese?

Yes — Llama 3.2 handles Vietnamese well. Just add your Vietnamese content to the knowledge base.
